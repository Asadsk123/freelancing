## DISCOVERY QUESTIONS (Ask 2-3 before proposing)

1. What's the actual business outcome you need? (Not the task — the result)
2. Who's your main user? What's their job?
3. What have you tried before that didn't work?
4. What does "done" look like to you in 3 months?
5. Are you looking for a vendor, or a long-term technology partner?
6. What's your biggest risk if this project fails?
7. Who else will be involved in evaluating the work?
8. What's your technical infrastructure right now? (Stack, databases, APIs)
9. Are you open to a phased approach, or do you need everything at once?
10. What's your decision timeline?

## OBJECTIONS & RESPONSES

**OBJECTION 1:** "You're more expensive than other freelancers."
**RESPONSE:** "I'm not cheaper because I'm not competing on price — I'm competing on outcome. The question isn't my hourly rate. It's: do you want someone who finishes 2 weeks early and ships scalable code, or someone cheaper who cuts corners and costs you time later?"

**OBJECTION 2:** "How long have you been doing this?"
**RESPONSE:** "5+ years shipping production systems. More importantly: I've made every mistake once, so you don't have to. [Specific reference to similar project completed]."

**OBJECTION 3:** "We've been burned by developers before."
**RESPONSE:** "That's exactly why I work in 2-week milestones with demos. You see working code every sprint. If direction is wrong, we pivot early — not at the end."

**OBJECTION 4:** "I need someone available immediately."
**RESPONSE:** "I can start [specific date]. For critical issues during development, I'm available same-day. My team has coverage for real emergencies."

**OBJECTION 5:** "Can you work with our existing team/codebase?"
**RESPONSE:** "Yes. Send me your stack. I've worked with [list: Next.js, React, Node.js, Python, Shopify, BigCommerce, whatever applies]. If it's custom code, I learn your architecture in the first week — no problem."

**OBJECTION 6:** "How do I know this will actually work?"
**RESPONSE:** "We build it in phases. First 2 weeks: core functionality + testing. You see it working before we go further. If it doesn't hit the mark, we iterate. You don't pay for a solution you don't trust."

**OBJECTION 7:** "I don't have time to manage this project."
**RESPONSE:** "I don't need much. Weekly 30-min sync + asynchronous feedback. I handle the technical decisions. You stay in the loop without living in Slack."

**OBJECTION 8:** "What if the scope changes?"
**RESPONSE:** "It will. That's normal. I build in 2-week sprints, so we adapt to scope changes without blowing the timeline. Extra scope = extra sprints. We discuss priority trade-offs as we go."

**OBJECTION 9:** "Why should I hire you instead of an agency?"
**RESPONSE:** "Agencies are slower (layers of management) and more expensive (overhead). You get one engineer who knows your project end-to-end. Faster decisions, faster shipping, direct communication."

**OBJECTION 10:** "How much will this actually cost?"
**RESPONSE:** "[Honest estimate based on scope]. That's the number if scope stays as discussed. I'll flag early if we're heading over. Prevents surprises at the end."
